Biography

Dr. Rasouli is the Director and Professor of the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UND. He previously served at The Pennsylvania State University Erie from 2013 to 2024, holding positions as Assistant, Associate, and Full Professor, as well as Department Chair of Electrical and Computer Engineering. With over 7 years of industry experience in power systems and control, Dr. Rasouli has worked as an Electrical Engineer at Jacobs Engineering, as well as a Project Manager and Research Scientist.

Courses: Electric Circuits I & II, Control Systems I & II, Electric Machines (Energy Conversion), Power Systems I & II, Power System Operation and Control, Power Electronics.

Research/Areas of Interest: Power system modeling, operation and control; Smart grids; Nonlinear system identification.

Credentials: P.E., Licensed Professional Engineer.

Education: Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering – University of Calgary, Canada.
