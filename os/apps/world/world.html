Scene Notes

Earth texture, bump, specular and night-light layers are pulled from the official Three.js texture set.

The atmosphere shell is rendered with a shader for a stronger edge glow than a flat transparent sphere.

Country borders are projected from live GeoJSON data so the globe reads closer to a true explorer surface.

Routes float above the globe and pulse brighter for the active location.
